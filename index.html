Regional Thermometer

Growth alone does not tell the story

The report compares seven indicators across 244 NUTS 2 regions and excludes missing values from progress calculations.

12% improved in six of seven indicators
Methodological note for specialist use

The seven indicators are GDP per capita in PPS, unemployment, poverty or social exclusion, inability to face unexpected expenses, emissions by sector, old-age dependency and the JRC Vulnerability Index. Comparisons use the two latest available years for each indicator.

Place-based

More places contribute. Investment, knowledge, skills and opportunity circulate across territories.

Five principles running through the report

  1. Preserve cohesion policy for all regions.
  2. Legally anchor partnership and multilevel governance.
  3. Apply a “do no harm to cohesion” principle.
  4. Use place-based territorial instruments.
  5. Integrate territorial impact assessments and regional checks.
